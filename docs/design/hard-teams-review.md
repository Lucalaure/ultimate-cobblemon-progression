# Hard teams review

The 40 Hard teams (13 Kanto, 13 Sinnoh, 14 Galar) checked against [hard-teams.md](hard-teams.md), November 2026. Types, abilities and base stats come from the Cobblemon 1.8.4 jar, and move types from Showdown's move data. Every Pokémon and move suggested below exists in Cobblemon and is in that species' learnset.

**The short version:** the sets themselves (abilities, items, natures, EVs, moves) are good throughout. The problems are mostly in *which* Pokémon was added: 13 added Pokémon share the team's weak spots instead of covering them, or outshine the ace. There is also one rule break (Sabrina) and three luck-based combos.

Verdicts:

- **Fix:** breaks a rule in hard-teams.md.
- **Swap:** the added Pokémon should be a different species.
- **Tweak:** the right species, but its set or a teammate's set needs a change.
- **Keep:** fits the rules.

"Weak spot 5/6" means that type hits 5 of the 6 team members super-effectively.

**Applied:** every Fix, Swap and Tweak below, Milo's optional swap included, but not the Smaller things or Kabu's optional Solar Beam. Where the review left a choice open: Misty's Horsea got Icy Wind, Erika's Victreebel got Weather Ball (Fire in sun, against the Ice types that beat her team), Lucian's Gallade a Black Belt, and Polteageist Cursed Body instead of Weak Armor, which only helped Shell Smash. Sabrina's Venomoth replaced Hypno at the same place in the team.

## Fix

### Sabrina

Her Normal team is Kadabra, Mr. Mime, **Venomoth**, Alakazam. The Hard team dropped Venomoth and added Hypno and Slowbro, so it breaks "every Normal Pokémon stays". It also loses her one off-type Pokémon: on Hard, Ghost hits all 5, and Bug and Dark hit 4 of 5.

**Change:** put Venomoth back instead of Hypno. Venomoth (Bug/Poison) resists Bug and isn't weak to Ghost or Dark. Hypno is the Pokémon to cut: it's a third Psychic type with no job the others don't do, and it brings a second sleep move (Hypnosis). A set like Tinted Lens, Black Sludge, Bug Buzz, Sludge Bomb, Toxic Spikes, Roost keeps Venomoth as support. Slowbro stays: Regenerator and Scald make it a good wall against physical Bug and Dark attackers.

## High priority

### Giovanni: Sandslash → Persian

Water and Ice hit **all 6** (Dugtrio, Marowak, Sandslash, Nidoqueen, Nidoking, Rhydon), and nothing resists either. A single Water or Ice type sweeps the fight. Sandslash adds nothing the other Ground types don't have.

**Change:** Persian, Giovanni's best-known partner. It's Normal, so neutral to Water and Ice, and as a pivot it brings Rhydon in safely: Technician, Sitrus Berry, Fake Out, U-turn, Knock Off, Body Slam. BST 440, below Rhydon's 485. Kangaskhan, from his FireRed Rocket Hideout and Silph Co. teams, works too (BST 490, a little above Rhydon's).

### Aaron: Scizor → Armaldo

Fire hits 5 of 6, and Scizor takes it 4×. Scizor (BST 500, Technician Bullet Punch, Swords Dance, Life Orb) is also as strong as Drapion, the ace, with the same kind of set.

**Change:** Armaldo (Rock/Bug, Gen 3 fossil). Fire and Flying hit it neutrally, and its Rock moves hit the Fire and Flying types that beat the rest of the team: Battle Armor, Leftovers, Stealth Rock, Rapid Spin, X-Scissor, Stone Edge. BST 495.

### Fantina: Dusclops → Sableye, and drop Minimize

Drifblim has **Minimize**, an evasion boost (not allowed). Ghost and Dark hit all 4, and Dusclops shares both.

**Change:**

- Drifblim: Minimize → Thunderbolt.
- Dusclops → Sableye (Dark/Ghost): neither Ghost nor Dark hits it super-effectively, and it does Dusclops's job faster. Prankster, Leftovers, Will-O-Wisp, Knock Off, Recover, Foul Play. BST 380.

### Opal: break the paraflinch

Togekiss has Serene Grace Air Slash (60% flinch) **and** Thunder Wave, and Klefki has Prankster Thunder Wave. A paralysed Pokémon facing Togekiss loses most of its turns to luck.

**Change:** Togekiss Thunder Wave → Nasty Plot. Klefki Thunder Wave → Foul Play. Klefki stays the screen setter.

### Bede: Sylveon → Klefki, and Gothitelle's ability

Sylveon (BST 525) is above Hatterene (510), and Choice Scarf Pixilate Hyper Voice makes it the team's strongest attacker. A fast Choice Scarf Pokémon also fights Hatterene's Trick Room, which makes slow Pokémon move first. Poison, Ghost and Steel each hit 4 of 6. Gothitelle has **Shadow Tag** and stalls with Calm Mind and Rest (trap and stall, not allowed).

**Change:**

- Sylveon → Klefki (Steel/Fairy): immune to Poison, and neutral to Steel and Ghost, so it covers all three weak spots. It fits, too: Bede takes over Opal's Fairy gym. Prankster, Light Clay, Reflect, Light Screen, Dazzling Gleam, Spikes. BST 470.
- Gothitelle: Shadow Tag → Competitive.

## Medium priority

### Brock: Rhyhorn → Kabuto

Every type that hits Rock/Ground hits all three, and Rhyhorn shares all of them. Kabuto (Rock/Water) takes Water, Ice and Steel neutrally, and Mega Drain fights back against Squirtle and Water types. In HeartGold/SoulSilver Brock uses Omastar and Kabutops. Swift Swim or Battle Armor, Eviolite, Mega Drain, Mud Shot, Aqua Jet, Rock Tomb. BST 355, below Onix's 385. Grass stays a weak spot, and that can't be avoided at the first gym.

### Misty: Psyduck → Tentacool; Horsea without Smokescreen

Grass and Electric hit all 4. Tentacool (Water/Poison) resists Grass and Fighting: Clear Body, Eviolite, Mega Drain, Water Pulse, Acid Spray, Toxic Spikes. Psyduck's set (Confusion, Disable, Water Pulse, Scratch) has no job anyway. Horsea's Smokescreen is an accuracy drop (not allowed): swap it for Bubble Beam or Icy Wind.

### Erika: Bellossom as the sun setter

Bellossom (BST 490, same as Vileplume) has Quiver Dance, Moonblast and Giga Drain, which makes it a stronger win condition than her ace. Erika's team runs on Chlorophyll, so give Bellossom the sun job instead: Heat Rock, Sunny Day, Giga Drain, Moonblast, Stun Spore. Then Victreebel can drop its own Sunny Day for another attack.

### Koga: Venomoth without the sleep sweep

Venomoth has Sleep Powder **and** Quiver Dance (sleep with a setup move, not allowed), and as a sweeper it outshines Weezing, his defensive ace. Make it support: Bug Buzz, Sludge Bomb, Toxic Spikes, Roost. Also give Golbat no item: Acrobatics doubles its power with an empty hand.

### Lorelei: one sleep move

Jynx has Lovely Kiss with Nasty Plot (sleep with setup), and Lapras has Sing: two sleep moves. Jynx: Nasty Plot → Focus Blast (it also hits the Rock and Steel types that beat the team). Lapras: Sing → Toxic. Mamoswine stays: it's immune to Electric (4/6) and fits, since Lorelei uses Piloswine in FireRed/LeafGreen's rematch.

### Agatha: Dusclops → Misdreavus; Mismagius as support

Dusclops is from an unrelated Gen 3 family, and Agatha doesn't use it in any game. Misdreavus, from her FireRed/LeafGreen rematch, does the same wall job with Eviolite: Will-O-Wisp, Pain Split, Hex, Shadow Ball. BST 435. Mismagius has Gengar's set (Nasty Plot, Shadow Ball, Thunderbolt) and competes with the ace. Give it support: Taunt, Thunder Wave, Pain Split, Shadow Ball, with an item. Psychic, Ghost and Dark still hit 4 of 6, but that comes with Ghost/Poison, and the team's Crunch and Shadow Balls punish them.

### Roark: Nosepass → Lileep

Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel hit all 4, and Nosepass shares all five. Its BST (375) is also above Cranidos's (350). Lileep (Rock/Grass) is a fossil, which suits a fossil collector. With Storm Drain it's immune to Water, and it takes Grass and Ground neutrally: Eviolite, Mega Drain, Ancient Power, Recover, Confuse Ray.

### Byron: Magneton → Skarmory, Bronzor with Heatproof

Magneton adds to all three weak spots (Fire, Fighting and Ground each 3/4, Ground 4× on Magneton). Skarmory (Steel/Flying) is immune to Ground and takes Fighting neutrally: Sturdy, Leftovers, Spikes, Roost, Whirlwind, Brave Bird. BST 465. With Skarmory covering Ground, Bronzor can switch Levitate for **Heatproof**, which halves Fire.

### Candice: Froslass → Piloswine

Fire, Rock and Steel hit 4 of 5, and Froslass shares all three. Piloswine (Ice/Ground) with **Thick Fat** takes Fire and Rock neutrally and is immune to Electric: Eviolite, Ice Shard, Icicle Crash, High Horsepower, Stealth Rock. BST 450, below Abomasnow's 494.

### Volkner: Electivire → Rotom

Electivire (BST 540) is above Luxray (523), and four attacks with Expert Belt make it the team's real ace. It also adds a third Ground weakness. Rotom (Electric/Ghost, Levitate) is immune to Ground and helps Luxray instead of competing with it: Leftovers, Volt Switch, Will-O-Wisp, Shadow Ball, Thunder Wave. BST 440.

### Lucian: Gallade's set

Gallade (BST 518) is above Bronzong (500), and Swords Dance plus Close Combat makes it the team's win condition. Ghost hits 5 of 6. Keep Gallade but change its set: Leaf Blade → Night Slash (hits Ghost and Psychic types), Swords Dance → Ice Punch, and give it an item.

### Allister: Polteageist as support

Polteageist (BST 508) is above Gengar (500), and Shell Smash, White Herb and Stored Power make it a sweeper that outshines the ace. Make it support: Leftovers, Strength Sap, Will-O-Wisp, Shadow Ball, Giga Drain.

### Gordie: Rhydon → Crustle

Water, Fighting and Ground hit 4 of 5. Rhydon shares all three and takes Water and Grass 4×. Crustle (Bug/Rock) takes Fighting, Ground and Grass neutrally, and X-Scissor hits the Grass types that beat the team: Sturdy, Spikes, Rock Blast, X-Scissor, Knock Off. BST 485. Shuckle already sets Stealth Rock, so Crustle sets Spikes.

### Marnie: Krookodile → Skuntank

Fairy hits 5 of 6, Fighting 4 and Bug 3, and Krookodile shares all three (its BST, 519, is also above Grimmsnarl's 510). Skuntank (Poison/Dark) takes all three neutrally and is her brother Piers's signature: Aftermath, Black Sludge, Sucker Punch, Crunch, Poison Jab, Toxic. BST 479.

## Low priority

### Milo: Nuzleaf → Roselia (optional)

Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug hit all 3, and Nuzleaf shares all five. Roselia (Grass/Poison) takes Poison and Bug neutrally and resists Fighting and Fairy: Natural Cure, Toxic Spikes, Magical Leaf, Sludge Bomb, Synthesis. It's the first gym, though, and a Grass gym's weakness is expected there, so this one is optional.

## Keep

- **Lt. Surge:** Ground hits all 4, but no Electric type in Gen 1 avoids that, and Raichu's Grass Knot punishes Ground types.
- **Blaine:** Water, Ground and Rock hit all 5, which comes with Fire. Ninetales's sun halves Water, its Solar Beam hits Water and Ground, and Magmar's Thunder Punch and Low Kick cover the rest.
- **Bruno:** Steelix and Golem resist the Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks that hit his four Fighting types.
- **Lance:** Kingdra is on his FireRed/LeafGreen rematch team, isn't weak to Ice, and stays below Dragonite.
- **Champion Blue:** the Normal team, well built. No type hits more than half.
- **Gardenia:** Fire, Ice and Flying hit all 4 (it's a Grass gym), but Tangela's Ancient Power punishes them.
- **Maylene:** Croagunk adds Psychic and Flying weaknesses, but its Sucker Punch and Poison Jab hit Psychic and Fairy types, and Machoke's Rock Tomb hits Flying ones.
- **Wake:** the model for the rules: Pelipper's Drizzle doubles Floatzel's speed with Swift Swim, and Quagsire is immune to Electric.
- **Bertha:** Gliscor covers Grass, which hits 5 of 6.
- **Flint:** Magmortar's Thunderbolt and Focus Blast hit the Water and Rock types that beat the team.
- **Champion Cynthia:** the Normal team, well built.
- **Nessa:** Pelipper's rain powers the Swift Swim team.
- **Kabu:** Torkoal's sun halves Water and its Stealth Rock wears the player down. Optional: Solar Beam on Ninetales, as Blaine's has.
- **Bea:** Flying and Fairy hit all 5, which comes with Fighting. Machamp's Rock Tomb and Bullet Punch, and the team's Knock Offs and Sucker Punches, answer them.
- **Melony:** Rock hits all 5, but no Galar Ice type avoids that without outshining Lapras. Surf, Earthquake and Liquidation answer the Rock types, and Abomasnow's snow keeps Eiscue's Ice Face.
- **Piers:** Toxtricity resists Fairy and hits it with Sludge Bomb.
- **Raihan:** no type hits more than 3 of 5, and Turtonator resists Ice.
- **Hop:** a mixed team; no type hits more than 2 of 6.
- **Champion Leon:** the Normal team, well built.

## Smaller things

These Pokémon hold no item without a reason for it (see step 6). Give each one that fits its set, keeping the ace's item the strongest:

- Kanto: Voltorb (Surge), Venomoth (Koga), Ninetales and Magmar (Blaine), Marowak (Giovanni; not a Thick Club, which would outshine Rhydon), Golem (Bruno), Mismagius (Agatha), Bellossom (Erika, Heat Rock above).
- Sinnoh: Onix (Roark), Magneton (Byron), Ambipom (Volkner), Sudowoodo (Bertha), Magmortar (Flint), Gallade (Lucian).
- Golbat (Koga) is the opposite: it holds a Sitrus Berry, but Acrobatics wants no item.
